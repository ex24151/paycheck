How it works

The workflow your finance team follows for every invoice.

  1. 1 · ReceiveInvoice arrives by email, upload, or accounting-system sync.
  2. 2 · CheckBank details, sender domain, amount history, wording, sanctions, plus a review by the AI analyst.
  3. 3 · ScoreReady to pay   Verify first   On hold
  4. 4 · VerifyCall the supplier on the number in your records. Red invoices also need a second person to approve.
  5. 5 · DecideApprove and pay, or block. Blocking adds the account to the fraud list so it can never be paid.
  6. 6 · AuditEvery step is logged with who, when and why, for auditors and insurers.
